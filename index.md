# Sobre — privacy policy

*Last updated: 23 November 2026* · [Version française](fr.md)

Sobre is a home screen (launcher) for Android. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app reads and where that data goes.

## Data read on the phone, never sent anywhere

| Data | Why | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| List of installed apps | Home grid, app list, search | none (package visibility for launcher apps) |
| App usage events (which app is in front, screen on/off, unlocks) | The screen time tile | Usage access, granted by you in Android's settings |
| Calendar events for today | The "today" page | Calendar |
| Contacts (names, phone numbers, starred contacts) | Contact search, call/text actions, favorites page | Contacts |
| Notifications from your messaging, mail and music apps (sender, text, subject, track, cover) | Message, mail and now-playing pages | Notification access, granted by you in Android's settings |

These are read when the screen needs them and kept only in the app's private storage on the phone (your home layout and settings). Nothing is copied to a server, shared with the developer or with anyone else. Backups of the app's data are turned off.

## Data sent off the phone

**Weather.** When you type a city in settings, its name is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to find it, and its coordinates (latitude and longitude of the city, not of your phone) are then sent to the Open-Meteo forecast service about once an hour. Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) is an open-source weather service; its own privacy policy applies to those requests: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms. The app never reads your phone's location. Clear the city to stop these requests.

**Web search.** When you choose "search the web" in search, your text is handed to the browser or search app you picked; that app's policy applies.

## Your choices

Every permission is optional. Without it, the matching tile or page stays empty and the rest of the launcher works. You can revoke any of them at any time in Android's settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

## Children

Sobre is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.
